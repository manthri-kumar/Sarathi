Research checked primary provider/product pages on 17 October 2026; it is not a hands-on competitor benchmark or a market-demand study. No prices, user counts or superiority metrics are asserted.

## Best-fit hypotheses

1. **Practical cultural visit companion.** Existing Temple Explorer, sourced historical text, local dishes and selected-place planning give SARATHI a plausible focus. Connect them into a short regional journey with reliable saved state and official practical facts. Why users might choose it: less work reconciling temple context, food and feasible timing across apps. Validation: travelers complete the same planning/resume task unassisted using Sarathi and their usual workflow; report actual completion/corrections, not aesthetic preference alone.
2. **Confidence through provenance and honest uncertainty.** Display source/time for operational facts, distinguish history from legend, estimated cost from actual fare, and unknown accessibility from absence. Why it matters: travel decisions have real time/money/access consequences. Validation: domain-reviewed factual fixtures and user ability to identify what is verified vs estimated. A21/A22 are prerequisites, not optional branding.
3. **Regional-language usable journey.** English/Telugu/Hindi scaffolding exists, but many key flows are English and keys are missing. Why it matters: a localized end-to-end cultural trip can serve users poorly supported by generic workflows. Validation: target-language participants search, plan, save, resume and recover an error without switching language or assistance. Don't claim full multilingual support from a language menu alone.
4. **Resume-first lightweight planning.** One durable plan accessible after logout/network interruption is a stronger retention mechanism than generating another list. Why it matters: travelers need the plan at the destination. Validation: cross-device resume and later optional offline read-only pilot; no data corruption during reconnect. Build only after A03–A05.

## Focus and non-goals

Recommended first audience hypothesis: regional temple/heritage travelers who want a short visit plan with nearby food and trustworthy guidance. Validate audience, geographic scope and factual maintenance capacity before broad expansion. Do not claim exclusive differentiation: competitors may support overlapping needs; no exhaustive market comparison was performed.

Defer payments/bookings, live rail comparisons, collaborative itineraries, a full admin CMS, trip social feeds, new AI personas and remote notification platforms until research demonstrates a missing user job. Existing dormant inbox/rail/RAG code should not expand launch scope merely because it exists. This reduces failure modes and directs effort to identity, durable plans, factual honesty and accessible journeys.

After repairs, proposed pilot funnel is discovery → correct place selection → useful feasible plan → acknowledged save → successful later resume. Measure drop-offs and factual corrections with informed instrumentation and data minimization. No acquisition/retention/monetization metric is available from this repository audit; no business model or willingness-to-pay claim is verified.
